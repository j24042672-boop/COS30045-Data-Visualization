Home energy overview

In the Australian market, appliance energy consumption is shaped by product size, operating time, climate, and usage habits. Larger appliances such as air conditioners and electric heating systems usually draw more power, while energy-efficient models can deliver similar performance with lower yearly energy use.

Monitoring power use and understanding daily operating hours helps households compare running costs and make better purchasing decisions.
